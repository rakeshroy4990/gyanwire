# Gyanwire — Brief

`Self-authored under explicit creative delegation`

The user asked for a website using the scrollcraft reference, with categories and a thoughts box that finds the best web results.
Creative direction for brand, structure, and polish was delegated by that ask.

## 1. Vibe

Calm tool. Forest green, bone type, amber accent. Clear, patient, useful.

References: a well-lit workbench, a library card catalog, a quiet status bar.

## 2. Scroll journey

This is a **live surface**, not a filmic marketing page. The product is the page.

1. Arrival on the working surface already open
2. Guide panel explains three plain steps
3. Compose panel: categories + thoughts
4. Results panel fills with ranked pages

## 3. Energy curve

Quiet throughout. Intensity lives in the results settling after search, not in
spectacle. The page should feel easy to understand in under ten seconds.

## 4. Feeling curve

1. Orientation — chrome and three steps make the tool obvious
2. Agency — picking categories and writing thoughts feels personal
3. Anticipation — live query preview shows what will be searched
4. Relief — ranked results arrive with plain “why” lines
5. Resolve — visitor opens a useful page

## Peak

> I typed a messy note, and the page came back with a short list that actually matched what I meant.

Act: results settle after Find best pages.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where you dump messy thoughts and get the best pages from the web, ranked for what you meant.

## 5. Signature move

**Likely search preview.** As categories and thoughts take shape, a dashed preview
shows the query about to run, before the visitor commits. That preview is the
bespoke interaction. It is not a kit device retune.

## 6. Aesthetic family

Premium-minimal tool surface. Deep forest ground, bone ink, single amber accent.
Display: Fraunces. Text: DM Sans.

## 7. Structure fork

Distinct live surface (operable product UI), not an unbroken worldflight.

## 8. Assets

No generated photography or video. The surface itself is the visual argument.
Typography, atmosphere gradients, and grain carry the craft floor from scrollcraft
taste rules without scrub clips.

## Grammar

Live surface. App chrome replaces marketing nav. Hero is the compose panel in a
ready state. Close is the thoughts field (cursorable input), not a magnetic CTA.
