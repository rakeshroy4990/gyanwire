# Gyanwire brief

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. The approved plan confirmed these decisions. They are not an interview transcript.

## What this is, and who it is for

Gyanwire turns a category and a messy thought, or a news item, into ranked findings and buildable ideas. The visitor is someone who would otherwise leave with a pile of links.

## What the visitor must believe

A finding is usable when it has a plain why and a next idea.

## The one action

`Start research`. One label, used for the only commitment on the page.

## Vibe

Dense, precise, live, quietly intense.

References, not sites: a night newsroom desk, a lab notebook, a tool that shows its work.

## Scroll journey

1. Already inside a research session.
2. The cost of an unexplained link pile.
3. A why-line.
4. One idea that can be opened.
5. An empty field that is theirs.

## Energy

Quiet, then tighter, then the peak, then a settle.

## Feeling curve

Written before the acts were scored.

1. Recognition. The surface is already working: a sample query sits in the field.
2. Unease. Links are present and none of them say why.
3. Relief. A why-line arrives on the same finding.
4. Focus. One idea opens. This is the peak.
5. Readiness. The field is empty and waiting.

No two adjacent lines share a feeling.

## The peak

The sentence a visitor would repeat: "The page opened one finding into an idea I could actually use."

It lives in act 4. That act gets the most scroll room. Act 3 is quieter than act 4.

## Tell-someone

It's the site where scrolling runs a research session, and the last thing on the page is the field where you start your own.

## Signature move

A wire that stays. Each accepted finding leaves a line on a fixed side rail. The lines remain after that moment passes, because they read the engine's `--sc-p` on the act that drew them, and at the close they move toward the caret in the field. Page script only. The engine is not edited.

## Range

Dense and information-forward. Not premium-minimal. Not cream, amber, and Fraunces.

## Structure

One product surface whose state changes. Not one continuous world. Distinct states, same tool.

## Assets

The real research UI and a labelled sample taken from the clinic-calibration fixture already in this repo. No brand film. No product photography. No generated video. No image API key.

## Grammar

Live surface.

The other seven lose:

- Filmic one-shot wants a carried film, a wordmark-and-CTA bar, and a scrub hero. This brief is a tool.
- Chaptered editorial wants a title page and a colophon. The visitor should operate something.
- Continuous world forbids sections and needs a world-flight. There is no place to fly through.
- Typographic poster makes type the subject. The subject is the running surface.
- Gallery is for objects you pick up.
- Split stage is a comparison.
- Rhythmic cutlist forbids the long hold this peak needs.

Bans held: no scrub, no kinetic type, no spotlight, no full-bleed photography, no display headline at poster size, no marketing header. Drift is unused, which is under the two-stop cap. The scenario is marked Sample on the surface. The close is the research field.

## Fingerprint gate

Registry at `scrollcraft/FINGERPRINTS.md` is empty. This first build has no prior row to clear.

## Score

Curve first. Devices second. Families: pin, pan, reveal, pin, flow. Four families. None twice in a row. No scrub. Peak span 4.6 is larger than the next span, 3.

| Beat | Device | Span | Why this one |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recognition | pin | 1.8 | The frame holds on a session that is already in progress. The query is the ground, so the stage is never empty. |
| Unease | pan | 3 | Lateral travel is the pile. Two findings, no why-line. About one viewport per item, plus one. |
| Relief | reveal | flow, one screen | A wipe is the change of state: the same finding grows a why. |
| Focus | pin | 4.6 | The idea opens while the frame holds. Longest span. The act before it is quieter. |
| Readiness | flow | the field | The page stops performing and offers the real input. The cue holds. Nothing follows it. |
