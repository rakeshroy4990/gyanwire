Anthropic launches fast and free Claude Haiku 5.5

Anthropic today launched Claude Haiku 5.5, a fast and free model aimed at developers and students who need quick answers.

Claude Haiku 5.5 is available at no cost for standard usage limits, according to the company announcement.

The model is designed for low-latency coding help and short writing tasks where speed matters more than deep reasoning.

Developers can try Claude Haiku 5.5 in the Anthropic console without a paid plan for the free tier.

Students are also listed among the early audiences who benefit from free access while learning to build with language models.

Anthropic said the launch expands the Claude family so teams can pick a cheaper, faster option for everyday prompts.

The company reiterated that Claude Haiku 5.5 focuses on responsiveness rather than replacing larger models for complex research.

Early docs show sample prompts for summarization, refactoring, and FAQ-style replies that finish in under a second on typical hardware paths.

Press materials emphasize that Claude Haiku 5.5 sits beside Sonnet and Opus, not above them, for production workflows.

Analysts covering the AI industry noted Anthropic is competing on free entry points while keeping paid tiers for higher volume.
