## Story (use this, don’t invent)

### What happened
U.S. consumers are expressing growing unease about the use of AI-assisted pricing tools by retailers and restaurant chains. These technologies, which include digital price tags and dynamic pricing algorithms, are leading to confusion and frustration among shoppers who are concerned about how their personal data is being used to set prices.

### Why it matters
This situation highlights significant tensions between consumers and companies regarding pricing transparency and data usage. As more retailers adopt these technologies, understanding their implications is crucial for consumers who want to protect their rights and finances.

### Key facts
1. Digital price tags are rapidly replacing paper ones in grocery aisles.
2. The Federal Trade Commission has proposed an enforcement policy warning businesses that failing to disclose their use of personal data to set individualized prices could violate consumer protection law.
3. Companies using the technology say some concerns stem from misconceptions about how their pricing tools work.
